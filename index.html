Payment terms

  • Balance due NET 15 from date of invoice, less deposit paid at signing
  • ACH preferred
  • 3% discount for payment received within 7 days of invoice
  • 5% late fee if not received within 30 days past due · additional 1.5% per month after 60 days
  • 4-hour minimum per guard, per shift
  • Extended-shift rate: hours beyond 8 per guard per shift are billed at 1.5× the hourly rate; additional hours require Client's prior written approval
  • Shifts on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, July 4th, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas and New Year's Eve are billed at 1.5× on all hours (not combined with the extended-shift rate)
  • Rush booking (under 48 hours' notice): 15% surcharge included in the total

Cancellation & refunds

Cancellation does not entitle Client to a full refund.

  • A deposit of 50% of the Estimated Total is due at signing to reserve this date, and is non-refundable once paid
  • Cancellation within 72 hours of start: full balance remains due — no refund
  • Cancellation beyond that window: deposit is retained; remaining balance is waived
  • Changes to guard count, hours, or posts require written approval and may change the total

Licensing, Insurance & Liability

Company licensing. Company holds a valid Washington State private security guard company license (Lic. No. ____________[WA license # pending]). All personnel assigned under this Agreement hold current, valid Washington unarmed private security guard licenses.

Insurance. Company maintains commercial general liability insurance — Carrier: ____________[Insurance carrier pending] · Policy No.: ____________[Policy # pending] · Coverage: ____________[Coverage pending] per occurrence. A certificate of insurance is available on request. Upon written request and subject to insurer approval, Client may be added as an additional insured.

Indemnification. Company shall indemnify and hold Client harmless from claims arising from the negligent acts or omissions of Company personnel in the performance of duties under this Agreement. Client shall indemnify and hold Company harmless from claims arising from undisclosed site hazards, Client's own negligence, or conditions outside Company's reasonable control. Company's total liability to Client under this Agreement, whether arising in contract, tort, or otherwise, shall not exceed the coverage limit of Company's commercial general liability insurance policy stated above.

Scope of authority. Officers are unarmed and serve to observe, deter and report. They do not make arrests except a citizen's arrest where Washington law allows it, use no force beyond what Washington law permits, do not pursue or chase anyone, and do not handle cash, valuables or weapons. In an emergency they call 911 and then notify Client. Company is an independent contractor, not an employee or agent of Client.

Patrol records. Officers use a guard-tour (checkpoint) app. Lion Security Services may place small QR checkpoint tags on the property and take photos for patrol and incident reports. These records are shared only with Client and kept confidential.

Term & Termination

    • Neither party is liable for delay or non-performance caused by events beyond that party's reasonable control, including severe weather, natural disaster, civil unrest, or government order. The cancellation terms above do not apply to such events; the parties will instead work in good faith to reschedule the affected coverage.

    Client Duties & Reporting

    • Client shall disclose known hazards and provide reasonable site access, lighting, and restroom facilities
    • Company shall submit written incident reports within 24 hours of any incident
    • Client shall not directly hire, or solicit for direct employment, any Company personnel assigned under this Agreement, for 12 months following termination — this restricts hiring Company's specific guards away; it does not restrict Client from engaging other security providers at any time
    • Any dispute arising from this Agreement shall first be resolved through binding arbitration in King County, Washington, under the American Arbitration Association's Commercial Arbitration Rules, before either party may pursue court action
    • Governed by the laws of the State of Washington; venue in King County
